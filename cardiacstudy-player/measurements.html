Measurements by modality

Every item on the app's Measure menu, by the kind of run it works on. An item is greyed out when the run on screen can't use it.

Every result is labelled "Educational use only". Keys are given for the Mac; on Windows, ⌥ (Option) is Alt, Return is Enter, and ⌥Tab is Ctrl+Tab. The Cardiology Training Guide (Mac, Nov 19, 2026; Windows, Nov 19, 2026) explains each one step by step.

Angiography (XA, RF)

Menu itemAvailable whenHow it's used
Measure ▸ StenosisAn XA or RF runDraw the reference diameter (R), the minimal lumen diameter (MLD), an optional second reference (R2) and the lesion length. Snap to Edges pulls each end onto the vessel wall. Gives % diameter and % area stenosis, with RVD, MLD and length in mm once calibrated (px before).
Measure ▸ LV Ejection FractionAn XA or RF runOn a ventriculogram, trace the LV outline at end-diastole, then at end-systole. Gives EDV and ESV (mL, when calibrated) and EF. One pair per run; ⌥Tab steps between the traces.
Measure ▸ Calibrate Caliper…Any image runSet the scale from a known size, usually the catheter (for example 6F): click its two edges and pick the size. It stays on the image as a Calibration mark and is listed in the Measurement Summary.

Echo: 2D images (US)

Menu itemAvailable whenHow it's used
Measure ▸ 2D Linear ▸ Left ventricle ▸ IVSd, LVIDd, LVPWd, LVIDsA US image, or any image with ultrasound regionsClick two points across the wall or cavity: IVSd, LVIDd and LVPWd at end-diastole, LVIDs at end-systole. Together they give LV mass (ASE cube), mass index, RWT, FS and Teichholz EF.
Measure ▸ 2D Linear ▸ Outflow ▸ LVOT diameter, Aortic rootThe sameLVOT inner edge to inner edge in mid-systole; aortic root at the sinuses at end-diastole. The LVOT diameter feeds stroke volume and AVA.
Measure ▸ 2D Area ▸ Atria ▸ LA (A4C), LA (A2C), RAThe sameTrace the atrium at end-systole (its largest) in the named view; Return or a double-click closes the trace. Gives LA volume (area-length) and LA volume index; A4C and A2C together give the biplane LA volume.
Measure ▸ 2D Area ▸ Right ventricle ▸ RV (ED), RV (ES)The sameTrace the RV in an RV-focused apical 4-chamber view at end-diastole and end-systole. Gives RV FAC (fractional area change).
Measure ▸ LV Volumes ▸ Apical 4-chamber / Apical 2-chamber ▸ LV A4C (ED), LV A4C (ES), LV A2C (ED), LV A2C (ES)The sameTrace the LV endocardium at end-diastole (largest) and end-systole (smallest). One view gives single-plane Simpson's EDV, ESV and EF; both views give the biplane Simpson's volumes and EF.

Echo: Doppler and M-mode (US)

Menu itemAvailable whenHow it's used
Measure ▸ Doppler ▸ Velocity ▸ MV E, MV A, AV Vmax, LVOT Vmax, TR Vmax, PV Vmax, e′ septal, e′ lateralThe image has a PW or CW Doppler stripClick the peak of the spectrum; Snap to Edges finds the envelope. Gives the velocity and peak gradient (tissue Doppler e′ in cm/s). Together they give E/A and E/e′.
Measure ▸ Doppler ▸ VTI ▸ LVOT VTI, AV VTI, MV VTIThe sameTrace the envelope of one beat (3 or more points, Return to finish). LVOT VTI with the LVOT diameter gives stroke volume, cardiac output and stroke volume index; with AV VTI or Vmax, AVA by continuity.
Measure ▸ Doppler ▸ Pressure half-time ▸ MV PHTThe sameClick the E peak, then a point down the deceleration slope. Gives the PHT and the mitral valve area (220 ÷ PHT).
Measure ▸ M-mode ▸ Annulus ▸ TAPSE, MAPSEThe image has an M-mode regionClick the annulus at end-diastole, then at end-systole.
Measure ▸ M-mode ▸ Left ventricle ▸ IVSd, LVIDd, LVPWd, LVIDsThe sameAs in 2D Linear, on the M-mode trace.
Measure ▸ M-mode ▸ Rhythm ▸ Heart rateThe sameClick two R waves (or the same point on two beats) one beat apart.
Measure ▸ M-mode ▸ Other ▸ Distance and timeThe sameAny two points: the distance and the time between them.

Every image run (XA, RF, US, CT, MR), and the whole study

Menu itemAvailable whenHow it's used
Measure ▸ Caliper ToolAny image run (not reports, ECG or other paged runs)Click two points for a distance, scaled by the ultrasound region, pixel spacing, the angiogram's geometry or your catheter calibration. On CT and MR it is the only measurement.
Measure ▸ Finish MeasurementA trace is in progressEnds a multi-point trace (LV, area, VTI); the same as Return.
Measure ▸ Snap to EdgesAngiograms and Doppler strips (not plain 2D echo)When on, points snap to vessel or catheter edges and to the Doppler envelope. Hold ⌥ to place a point exactly.
Measure ▸ Measurement Summary…A study is openEvery measurement in the study and where it was made, the mean of repeated ones (never of stenoses), and results that combine images: biplane LA volume and Simpson's EF, stroke volume, cardiac output, AVA and E/e′. Copy it as a table or export CSV, with no patient details.
