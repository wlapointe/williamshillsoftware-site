CardiacStudy Player

Cath, Echo & CT DICOM Viewer

Play cath and echo cines at their acquired frame rate, with the study's reports and ECG in the same window. Opens patient CDs offline. One purchase, no subscription.

Features

Play it as acquired

  • Angiography and fluoroscopy at their recorded frame rate
  • Step frame by frame, change the speed, set start and end frames
  • DSA subtraction with your choice of mask frame and pixel shift
  • Window/level, presets and edge enhancement
  • Zoom and pan at full resolution

Echo, CT & MR

  • Echo clips, video and still images
  • CT and MR series you step through slice by slice
  • Calipers calibrated from the ultrasound region or to catheter size
  • Reads current DICOM compression, including JPEG XL

ECG & waveforms

  • 12-lead ECG and hemodynamic waveforms drawn as calibrated lead strips
  • Paged like a document, right beside the imaging

Reports & teaching

  • Structured reports, PDF reports and presentation states, paged like documents
  • Quiz Mode: hide the report, read the images cold, then compare
  • Export QuickTime movies and still frames for lectures
  • Crop burned-in patient details out of exported images
Open any study

Patient CDs, DICOMDIR folders or loose DICOM files. Every run, report and waveform in one strip, in one window.

Private by design

Works offline with no account. The app cannot use the network, and nothing leaves your Mac.

One purchase

Paid once on the Mac App Store, with free updates. No subscription and no in-app purchases.

Not for clinical use. CardiacStudy Player is not a certified medical device and must not be used for diagnosis or clinical decisions. Read and report from your clinical PACS or cath lab workstation.

Support

Requirements

  • macOS 15 or later
  • Apple silicon or Intel Mac
  • No account and no internet connection needed

What can it open?

Patient CDs, DICOMDIR folders, folders of DICOM files and single DICOM files. The DICOM Conformance Statement lists every supported image type and compression.

Why does it ask me to choose a folder after I open a DICOMDIR file?

Mac App Store apps may read only what you choose. A DICOMDIR file only indexes the study; the images sit in the folders around it. The app asks once for the disc or folder that holds the DICOMDIR (it is already selected), so click Open. To skip this step, open the disc or folder itself instead of the DICOMDIR file.

Does it send my studies anywhere?

No. The app cannot use the network, and nothing leaves your Mac. See the privacy policy.

A study won't open, or something looks wrong

Email us with the modality (for example XA, US or CT), the equipment vendor if you know it, what you expected and what you saw. A screenshot of the app with patient details cropped out helps. Please never send patient images, discs or files that identify a patient.
