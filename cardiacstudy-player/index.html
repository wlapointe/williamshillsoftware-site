CardiacStudy: DICOM Viewer

Cath, Echo, CT & MR Player for Mac and Windows

Mac App Store: version 1.1.0 · Microsoft Store: version 1.2.1 (CardiacStudy Player) · What's new

Play cath and echo cines at their acquired frame rate, with the study's reports and ECG in the same window. Opens patient CDs offline. One purchase, no subscription.

Features

Play it as acquired

  • Angiography and fluoroscopy at their recorded frame rate
  • Step frame by frame, change the speed, set start and end frames
  • DSA subtraction with your choice of mask frame and pixel shift
  • Window/level, presets and edge enhancement
  • Zoom and pan at full resolution

Echo

  • Echo clips, video and still images
  • Calipers calibrated from the ultrasound region or to catheter size
  • Reads current DICOM compression, including JPEG XL

Angio measurements (new in 1.2)

  • Stenosis: reference and minimal lumen diameters that snap to the vessel edges, a second reference and lesion length
  • % diameter and % area stenosis, in millimetres once the caliper is calibrated on the catheter
  • LV ejection fraction from end-diastolic and end-systolic ventriculogram traces
  • Text, arrows, boxes and ovals, kept with the study and burned into copies and exports
  • Every result is labelled "Educational use only"

All measurements by modality

Echo measurements (new in 1.2)

  • LV volumes and EF by Simpson's method, single-plane or biplane across two clips
  • Doppler velocities, VTI and pressure half-time, with E/A and E/e′
  • 2D dimensions and areas, LV mass, LA volume and M-mode
  • Each calibrated by the ultrasound region it is drawn in
  • A measurement summary for the whole study, copied as a table or exported as CSV, with no patient details from the images

All measurements by modality

Doppler (new in 1.2)

  • Peak velocities and gradients, VTI and pressure half-time on PW and CW Doppler strips
  • E/A and E/e′ worked out for you
  • Calibrated from the Doppler region's own velocity scale, so no manual setup
  • The pointer is a crosshair wherever a click places a point

Measurement summary (new in 1.2)

  • Every measurement in the study in one window, with where each was made
  • The mean of repeated measurements (never of stenoses)
  • Study results that combine images, such as biplane EF, stroke volume and aortic valve area
  • A catheter calibration is listed too, so you can see what a run's lengths are based on
  • Copy as a table or export CSV, with no patient details from the images

CT & MR

  • CT and MR series you step through slice by slice
  • Each slice's body position, for example "S 42.5 mm"
  • A reference view on the study's own scout image: click or drag it to jump to any level
  • Mark landmark slices (Aortic root, Left main, LAD, Apex or your own names) and jump back to them

ECG & waveforms

  • 12-lead ECG and hemodynamic waveforms drawn as calibrated lead strips
  • Paged like a document, right beside the imaging

Reports & teaching

  • Structured reports, PDF reports and presentation states, paged like documents
  • Quiz Mode: hide the report, read the images cold, record your findings with the measurements behind them, then compare
  • Export QuickTime movies and still frames for lectures
  • Crop burned-in patient details out of exported images

Support

Requirements

  • Mac: macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel
  • Windows: Windows 10 (version 1809 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit (x64)
  • No account and no internet connection needed

Once installed, the app appears as CardiacStudy Player: in your Applications folder and menu bar on a Mac, and in the Start menu on Windows. On the Microsoft Store it is listed as CardiacStudy Player.

What can it open?

Patient CDs, DICOMDIR folders, folders of DICOM files and single DICOM files. The DICOM Conformance Statement (Mac, Windows; see Documentation) lists every supported image type and compression.

Mac: why does it ask me to choose a folder after I open a DICOMDIR file?

Mac App Store apps may read only what you choose. A DICOMDIR file only indexes the study; the images sit in the folders around it. The app asks once for the disc or folder that holds the DICOMDIR (it is already selected), so click Open. To skip this step, open the disc or folder itself instead of the DICOMDIR file.

Does it send my studies anywhere?

No. The app makes no network connections, and nothing leaves your Mac or PC. See the privacy policy.

A study won't open, or something looks wrong

Email us with the modality (for example XA, US or CT), the equipment vendor if you know it, what you expected and what you saw. A screenshot of the app with patient details cropped out helps. Please never send patient images, discs or files that identify a patient.
