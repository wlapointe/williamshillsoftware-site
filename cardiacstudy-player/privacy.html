Privacy Policy

CardiacStudy: DICOM Viewer (Mac) and CardiacStudy Player (Windows) · Last updated 17 November 2026 (the Windows app added; the policy is unchanged)

This policy covers CardiacStudy: DICOM Viewer on the Mac App Store and CardiacStudy Player on the Microsoft Store. Neither app collects, stores or shares any information about you or the studies you open. Everything the app does happens on your computer.

No data leaves your computer

The app makes no network connections and contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services. On the Mac it has no permission to use the network at all.

Your purchase and, on Windows, the 30-day free trial are handled by the Mac App Store or the Microsoft Store, under Apple's or Microsoft's own privacy policies. The app receives only whether it is licensed; it never sees your account or payment details.

Your studies

The app reads only the CDs, folders and files you choose to open, and it does not copy them or change them. Exported movies, images, findings and measurement tables are saved only where you choose to save them.

What the app keeps on your computer

  • Your display preferences, such as font size and highlight colour.
  • Annotations, measurements, catheter calibrations and slice marks you make, so they are there when you open the study again. They are filed under each image's DICOM identifier, never written into the DICOM files, and hold only what you draw or type.
  • Quiz Mode templates you create or edit.

These stay on your computer. Deleting the app removes them.

Contacting the developer

If you email the developer, your message is used only to reply to you. Questions about this policy: support@williamshillsoftware.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
