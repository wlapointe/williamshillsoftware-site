Privacy Policy

CardiacStudy Player · Last updated 21 October 2026

CardiacStudy Player does not collect, store or share any information about you or the studies you open. Everything the app does happens on your Mac.

No data leaves your Mac

The app has no permission to use the network. It cannot send anything anywhere, and it contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.

Your studies

The app reads only the CDs, folders and files you choose to open, and it does not copy them. Exported movies and images are saved only where you choose to save them.

What the app keeps on your Mac

  • Your display preferences, such as font size and highlight colour.
  • Quiz Mode templates you create or edit.

These stay on your Mac. Deleting the app removes them.

Contacting the developer

If you email the developer, your message is used only to reply to you. Questions about this policy: support@williamshillsoftware.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
